# SYK OS

A playbook for [AME Wizard](https://ameliorated.io) that makes Windows 10 and 11 lighter and replaces the taskbar, Start, and Windows menus with **SYK Island**, a dynamic island for Windows.

SYK Island and SYK Tools are in English by default; Bahasa Indonesia can be chosen in Settings.

## Download

**[SYK-OS.apbx (latest)](https://github.com/dansyaku7/sykos/releases/latest/download/SYK-OS.apbx)** · [All releases](https://github.com/dansyaku7/sykos/releases)

Supported: Windows 10 22H2, Windows 11 23H2, 24H2, 25H2.

## Install

1. Download AME Wizard from ameliorated.io and open it.
2. Drag `SYK-OS.apbx` into AME Wizard.
3. Temporarily turn off Defender protection (required by every AME playbook).
4. Pick your options and wait about 10 minutes. Windows restarts on its own.

To upgrade from an older SYK OS, load the new `.apbx`, choose **Yes** to overwrite, click **Upgrade**, then **Next**. Your SYK Island settings are kept.
